# Case Study Scenarios to discuss

## Scenario 1: Cost Allocation and Tracking
**Situation**: The company needs to track and allocate costs accurately across different Warehouses and Stores. The costs include labor, inventory, transportation, and overhead expenses.

**Task**: Discuss the challenges in accurately tracking and allocating costs in a fulfillment environment. Think about what are important considerations for this, what are previous experiences that you have you could related to this problem and elaborate some questions and considerations

**Questions you may have and considerations:**
[ I'd start by understanding the main cost components like labor, inventory, transportation and overhead, and how each should be allocated between Warehouses and Stores. The important part would be having clear allocation rules, keeping costs traceable, and handling shared costs properly. I'd also track costs over time to compare locations and identify where the major costs are coming from. Before designing the solution, I'd then clarify the source of the cost data, which system is the source of truth, how often it needs to be updated, and what reporting the business expects. The goal would be to have reliable and transparent cost data that helps the business understand where money is being spent and make better decisions. ]

## Scenario 2: Cost Optimization Strategies
**Situation**: The company wants to identify and implement cost optimization strategies for its fulfillment operations. The goal is to reduce overall costs without compromising service quality.

**Task**: Discuss potential cost optimization strategies for fulfillment operations and expected outcomes from that. How would you identify, prioritize and implement these strategies?

**Questions you may have and considerations:**
[ I'd first identify the major cost drivers and understand where we have opportunities to reduce costs without affecting service quality. I'd look at areas such as transportation, inventory levels, warehouse operations and resource utilization. The improvements should be prioritized based on their expected cost savings, business impact and implementation effort. I would then implement the highest value changes first and track the results to make sure the expected savings are actually achieved. ]

## Scenario 3: Integration with Financial Systems
**Situation**: The Cost Control Tool needs to integrate with existing financial systems to ensure accurate and timely cost data. The integration should support real-time data synchronization and reporting.

**Task**: Discuss the importance of integrating the Cost Control Tool with financial systems. What benefits the company would have from that and how would you ensure seamless integration and data synchronization?

**Questions you may have and considerations:**
[ I'll first understand which financial systems are involved and what data needs to be exchanged between them. The integration should provide accurate and timely cost data while avoiding duplicate or inconsistent records. I'd also consider reliable data synchronization, error handling, and monitoring so that integration issues can be identified quickly. The goal would be to have consistent financial data that supports accurate reporting and decision-making.]

## Scenario 4: Budgeting and Forecasting
**Situation**: The company needs to develop budgeting and forecasting capabilities for its fulfillment operations. The goal is to predict future costs and allocate resources effectively.

**Task**: Discuss the importance of budgeting and forecasting in fulfillment operations and what would you take into account designing a system to support accurate budgeting and forecasting?

**Questions you may have and considerations:**
[ I'd first understand the historical costs, expected demand, inventory levels and operational requirements to build a realistic budget. The system should allow actual costs to be compared with the planned budget and highlight significant differences. Forecasts should be updated regularly based on changing demand, costs and business conditions. The goal would be to identify potential cost overruns early and help the business plan resources more effectively. ]

## Scenario 5: Cost Control in Warehouse Replacement
**Situation**: The company is planning to replace an existing Warehouse with a new one. The new Warehouse will reuse the Business Unit Code of the old Warehouse. The old Warehouse will be archived, but its cost history must be preserved.

**Task**: Discuss the cost control aspects of replacing a Warehouse. Why is it important to preserve cost history and how this relates to keeping the new Warehouse operation within budget?

**Questions you may have and considerations:**
[ I'd first compare the replacement costs with the existing Warehouse’s operating costs and budget. The old Warehouse’s cost history should be preserved so we can track past spending and compare it with the new Warehouse. We also consider one-time replacement costs, expected savings, and any impact on operations. The goal would be to ensure the replacement stays within budget while giving the business a clear view of its financial impact ]
